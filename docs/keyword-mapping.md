# Explore India — Keyword Research & Mapping Document (CSET489)

## 1. Strategy Overview (Zero Keyword Cannibalization)

Every URL on **Explore India** is mapped to **one distinct primary search intent and unique target keyword**, strictly eliminating cannibalization between destination hubs, regional/category collections, and long-tail blog articles. All `<title>` tags are kept under 60 characters (`49–59 chars`) to avoid Google SERP truncation.
